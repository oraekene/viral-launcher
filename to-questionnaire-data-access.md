# What We Need From You To Connect Real Data

**Purpose:** The app can already score drafts, rewrite them, and track launches — but right now it learns from fake example data. To make it learn from *your* real posts, we need a few simple choices from you.

**From:** the builder — **To:** you (the owner) — **How your answers will be used:** Your answers will be saved as the app's settings. No more fake data — real scores, real warnings.

## Context

The app has 3 parts that are waiting on you. (1) It needs your real post results to get smarter. (2) It needs to know if X is quietly limiting your account. (3) It needs to know what you want to pay for and how often it should run. Everything below is written in plain language. If you don't know an answer, just write "I don't know".

## How to answer

Try to answer within the next few days. It should take 15-20 minutes. You can answer some now and some later. Short answers are fine. A one-line answer is better than no answer.
